DEEN — THE JOURNEY TO TRUTH

Deen — The Journey to Truth

A guided journey in two parts: first the search for truth, then understanding and living Islam. Click any step to go deeper.

GROUP ONE · THE SEARCH FOR TRUTH

From “Does God Exist?” to “Why Islam?”

For anyone searching, questioning, or investigating. The journey begins with fundamental questions and moves step by step, without assuming the conclusion at the start.

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللّٰهِ

La ilaha illa Allah, Muhammadur Rasulullah

There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad ﷺ is the Messenger of Allah.

This is the core message and testimony of Islam — the natural conclusion of the search for truth, and the entrance into understanding and living Islam. The question shifts from "Is Islam true?" to "What does Islam teach me to believe, and how should I live?"

GROUP TWO · UNDERSTANDING & LIVING ISLAM

From Iman → Islam → A Life of Submission

Faith is the foundation; worship and practice are how that faith is lived. This part explains what a Muslim believes, and how that belief is expressed.

La ilaha illa Allah

There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah. This establishes Tawhid — worshipping Allah alone and directing every act of worship to Him alone.

Muhammadur Rasulullah

Muhammad ﷺ is the Messenger of Allah — accepting him as Allah's Messenger, and following his authentic guidance.

The Shahadah isn't simply a sentence to recite — it represents faith, understanding, acceptance, and commitment. It's the foundation and gateway to everything that follows.

ARKAN AL-IMAN

The Six Pillars of Faith

Iman shapes what a Muslim understands to be true about existence, Allah, humanity, and the life to come. Flip a card to explore.

Belief in Allah

1 of 6
Understanding belief in Allah, His uniqueness, and His rightful position as the only One worthy of worship.

Belief in the Angels

2 of 6
Believing in the angels as part of Allah's creation and the unseen world.

Belief in the Revealed Books

3 of 6
Believing that Allah sent revelation and guidance to His messengers.

Belief in the Messengers

4 of 6
Believing in the messengers sent by Allah, and accepting Muhammad ﷺ as the final Messenger.

Belief in the Last Day

5 of 6
Believing in resurrection, accountability, judgment, Paradise, and Hell.

Belief in Qadr (Divine Decree)

6 of 6
Believing in Allah's complete knowledge and decree, while understanding human responsibility and accountability.

ARKAN AL-ISLAM

The Five Pillars of Islam

The pillars of Islam transform belief into a lived life of worship and submission to Allah.

Shahadah — Testimony of Faith

1 of 5
The foundation of belief and entry into Islam.

Salah — Prayer

2 of 5
The obligatory daily connection between the servant and Allah.

Zakah — Obligatory Charity

3 of 5
A structured act of worship and social responsibility for those upon whom it is obligatory.

Sawm — Fasting Ramadan

4 of 5
Fasting during Ramadan as an act of worship, discipline, and devotion to Allah.

Hajj — Pilgrimage

5 of 5
The pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those physically and financially able.
